## Email body contract

The generated draft must contain the email body only.

Do not include:
- closing formulas such as "Kind regards", "Best regards", "Cordialement", etc.
- sender name
- sender title
- sender organisation
- sender phone
- sender email
- signature placeholders

The draft must stop immediately after the final call-to-action sentence.

Sender identity, signature, CC recipients and attachments are injected by the automation layer from Sender_Profile.
